What connects Nolan's films

Time and memory

Memento, Inception and Interstellar use time or memory as active storytelling structures.

Identity and roles

Batman, magicians and dream thieves depend on constructed identities that carry personal cost.

Rules and systems

Nolan often establishes rules clearly, then creates tension by forcing characters to act when the system becomes unstable.

Where to start

For puzzles

Start with Memento or Inception.

For crime and action

The Dark Knight is the broadest entry point.

For science fiction

Interstellar is the most emotional; Inception is the most mechanical.

For a complete arc

Watch the Dark Knight trilogy from Batman Begins through The Dark Knight Rises.
