Quentin Tarantino

The current MovieAnchor index shows three distinct stages of Tarantino’s style: the contained suspicion of Reservoir Dogs, the nonlinear underworld sprawl of Pulp Fiction and the dialogue-driven alternate history of Inglourious Basterds.

What connects these films

Dialogue as action

Conversations are often the main source of tension rather than pauses between events.

Performance and identity

Characters survive by playing roles, hiding information or reading other people.

Flexible chronology

Order changes when rearranging events can alter how the audience interprets a character.

Where to start

Start with Pulp Fiction for the broadest example of Tarantino’s style. Choose Reservoir Dogs if you prefer a smaller crime thriller. Pick Inglourious Basterds if you want longer suspense sequences and historical revision.
