The Godfather

What stands out

Family as institution

Personal loyalty and organizational power become difficult to separate.

Transformation

The story gains force because Michael’s change happens through understandable decisions rather than one sudden turn.

Ritual and power

Meetings, favors and ceremonies make authority visible even before violence occurs.

Where to start

Start with The Godfather. If you like its patient institutional storytelling, compare it with Goodfellas for a faster insider view of organized crime or with Parasite for a very different family story where class and power are inseparable.
