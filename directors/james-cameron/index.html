What stands out

Readable action

Complex set pieces remain easy to follow because location, objectives and consequences are clear.

Technology and humanity

Machines, systems and engineering repeatedly become tests of human judgment.

Scale with personal stakes

Large spectacle is tied to simple emotional goals.

Where to start

Start with Terminator 2 for Cameron at his most complete as an action filmmaker, The Terminator for the leanest version of his style, Aliens for military science fiction, or Titanic for a very different emotional epic.
