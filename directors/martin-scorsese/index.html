What stands out

Subjective perspective

Both films keep viewers close to characters whose interpretation of the world cannot be treated as neutral.

Crime as environment

Violence is connected to social systems, identity and belonging rather than isolated events.

Style and psychology

Editing, music, voice-over and camera movement reflect how the protagonist experiences the world.

Where to start

Start with Goodfellas if you want speed, voice-over and criminal-world immersion. Choose Taxi Driver for a slower, more disturbing character study.
