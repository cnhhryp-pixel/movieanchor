Movies like Arrival

People often look for movies like Arrival for different reasons: the first-contact premise, the quiet tone, the language focus, the nonlinear experience of time or the emotional use of science fiction. These picks are grouped by that intent.

Pick by what you liked most

First contact and science

Contact is the strongest companion if evidence, institutions and communication interested you most.

Time and emotion

Interstellar is larger and more spectacular, but it also makes time a direct emotional problem.

Communication with nonhuman intelligence

Her turns that idea inward through intimacy rather than first contact.

Quiet, controlled science fiction

Ex Machina offers a smaller setting with language, intelligence and trust at the center.
