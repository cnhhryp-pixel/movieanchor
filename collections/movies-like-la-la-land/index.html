Movies like La La Land

La La Land is not only a musical romance; it is also a story about timing, ambition and whether a relationship can remain the same while both people keep changing. These picks continue those different ideas.

Pick by what you liked most

Romance shaped by timing

Before Sunrise is the best next watch if temporary connection and timing mattered most.

Love and change

Her is quieter and more speculative but equally focused on accepting that relationships evolve.

Memory and loss

Eternal Sunshine of the Spotless Mind turns regret and attachment into science-fiction romance.

Artistic ambition

Whiplash is the clearest Damien Chazelle companion if career obsession interested you most.
