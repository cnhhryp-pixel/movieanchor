Mind-bending movies

What makes the puzzle satisfying

The strongest puzzle films are not confusing simply to hide information. Their structure reflects what the story is about. Memento feels fragmented because its protagonist cannot retain new memories. Arrival changes chronology because the story is about experiencing time differently. The Matrix destabilizes reality because discovering the system is the character’s central problem.
