Time travel movie starting points

Time-travel movies use the idea in very different ways. Some are about changing history, some about repeating experience, and others use time itself to reshape emotion or destiny.

Pick the kind of time travel you want

Changing the past

Back to the Future is the most accessible starting point.

Fate and prevention

The Terminator and Terminator 2 ask whether the future can be changed.

Repeating time

Groundhog Day uses repetition for comedy and character growth.

Relativity and nonlinear experience

Interstellar and Arrival use time in more emotional and conceptual ways.

How the subgenres differ

Back to the Future treats time as a system of cause and effect. Terminator uses it to create a conflict between fate and agency. Groundhog Day removes technical explanation and focuses on character change. Interstellar and Arrival use time less like a machine and more like a way of changing how relationships are experienced.
