2000s movie starting points

The 2000s produced some of MovieAnchor’s strongest long-form franchises, animated landmarks, crime dramas and nonlinear thrillers. This page is a curated route rather than a ranking.

How to use this list

If you want a puzzle, start with Memento. For spectacle, choose Gladiator or The Fellowship of the Ring. For animation, Spirited Away and WALL·E represent two very different approaches. The Dark Knight is the most accessible crime-action pick.
