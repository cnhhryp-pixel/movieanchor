2010s movie starting points

This is a curated route through the 2010s rather than a ranking. Each pick represents a different strength of the decade: high-concept science fiction, intense performance drama, modern horror, romance, action and international social thriller.

How to use this list

If you want the most accessible blockbuster, start with Inception. For an intimate character film, choose Whiplash. For pure visual action, Mad Max: Fury Road is the clearest pick. Arrival is the best choice for thoughtful science fiction, Get Out for horror, and Parasite for a film that moves between comedy, suspense and tragedy.
