Movies like Blade Runner 2049

Blade Runner 2049 stands out because it combines a detective story with slow pacing, huge visual spaces, artificial life and questions about memory. The best next movie depends on whether you want more atmosphere, AI or identity.

Pick by what you liked most

The original world

Blade Runner is essential if you want the source of the sequel’s world and themes.

Artificial intelligence

Ex Machina is smaller, sharper and more focused on manipulation and personhood.

Human-AI relationships

Her approaches artificial intelligence through intimacy rather than noir or violence.

Cyberpunk action

The Matrix is faster and more accessible while still exploring identity and systems.
