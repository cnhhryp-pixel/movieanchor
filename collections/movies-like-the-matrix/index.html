Movies like The Matrix

The Matrix combines several pleasures at once: questioning reality, cyberpunk technology, action built around world rules and a protagonist discovering a hidden system. The best follow-up depends on which part you want more of.

AI and manipulation

Ex Machina turns intelligence and control into a small psychological thriller.

Technology and fate

Terminator 2 offers a more direct action-driven approach.
