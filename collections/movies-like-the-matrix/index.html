Movies like The Matrix

The Matrix combines several pleasures at once: reality-questioning science fiction, martial-arts action, cyberpunk design, hidden systems and a protagonist discovering that his ordinary life is built on a lie. The best follow-up depends on which part you want more of.

Pick by what you liked most

Reality-bending action

Inception is the closest modern blockbuster match.

Artificial intelligence and control

Ex Machina is much smaller but sharper on manipulation and personhood.

Cyberpunk atmosphere

Blade Runner is slower and more noir-driven, but foundational for identity-focused science fiction.

Technology, fate and action

Terminator 2 offers another landmark action film about human agency under technological threat.
