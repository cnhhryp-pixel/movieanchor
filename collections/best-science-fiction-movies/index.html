Science fiction starting points

This is not a ranking of all science-fiction movies. It is a practical starting guide organized around different experiences: reality-bending concepts, space and survival, first contact, artificial intelligence and political world-building.

Pick by subgenre

Reality-bending

The Matrix and Inception are the easiest entry points.

Space and survival

Interstellar is emotional; The Martian is practical.

First contact

Arrival is language-driven; Contact is science-and-institutions driven.

Artificial life

Blade Runner 2049, Ex Machina and Her each take a different approach.

How to choose

If you want speed and action, choose The Matrix. If you want complexity, Inception. If you want emotion, Interstellar. For quiet ideas, Arrival. For atmosphere, Blade Runner 2049. For world-building and politics, Dune.
