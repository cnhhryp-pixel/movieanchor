How to choose your next movie

If you want something fast and accessible, start with The Matrix or Inception. If you prefer slower, more reflective science fiction, choose Arrival or Blade Runner 2049. Interstellar works well for viewers who want emotion and scale, while Dune is the strongest choice for political world-building.
