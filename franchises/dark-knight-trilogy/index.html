The Dark Knight trilogy in order

Christopher Nolan’s Batman trilogy is designed to be watched in release order. Each film changes Bruce Wayne’s problem: becoming a symbol, facing the consequences of that symbol, and deciding whether the symbol can outlast him.

How the trilogy evolves

Origin

Batman Begins asks why Bruce creates the symbol and what separates justice from revenge.

Escalation

The Dark Knight shows what happens when an extraordinary symbol changes the behavior of criminals and institutions.

Legacy

The Dark Knight Rises asks whether Batman can become larger than the individual maintaining the role.

Best place to start

Do not skip Batman Begins if this is your first watch. The Dark Knight can stand alone as a crime thriller, but the trilogy’s larger argument about fear, symbols and Bruce Wayne’s choices is much clearer when seen from the beginning.
