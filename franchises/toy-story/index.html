Toy Story movies in order

How the emotional arc develops

Belonging

The first film defines friendship and status inside Andy’s room.

Being outgrown

The second and third films turn the end of childhood into the series’ central fear.

Purpose beyond a role

The fourth film focuses specifically on Woody after his original job has changed.

Why release order matters

Toy Story 4 makes much more sense after the emotional ending of Toy Story 3. Likewise, Toy Story 2 introduces the fear of being outgrown long before the third film turns it into reality.
