Toy Story movies in order

The four main Toy Story films are best watched in release order because Woody’s character arc depends on gradual change. The series begins with fear of being replaced and eventually asks what identity looks like when the role that defined you is no longer necessary.

How Woody's story changes

Status

The first film is about fear of losing a privileged place in someone else’s life.

Attachment

The sequels gradually separate being owned from being valued.

Purpose

The fourth film asks who Woody is when service to an owner is no longer his only identity.

Best entry for different viewers

Best starting point

Toy Story remains the essential first film because it establishes Woody, Buzz and the rules of the toy world.

Most emotional

Toy Story 3 has the strongest focus on growing up and transition.

Most reflective

Toy Story 2 and 4 spend more time on what a toy’s future and purpose might mean.

For animation history

The original is also a major milestone as the first fully computer-animated feature film.
