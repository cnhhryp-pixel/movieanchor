The Lord of the Rings trilogy in order

The core trilogy is designed as one continuous story, so release order is also story order. Start with Fellowship, continue through The Two Towers and finish with The Return of the King.

How the trilogy grows

Quest

Fellowship focuses on leaving home and accepting a burden.

Division

The Two Towers shows how shared purpose survives physical separation.

Return and change

Return of the King asks what victory means for people who cannot simply go back to who they were.

Why not skip the first film

The trilogy’s emotional weight comes from accumulation. Friendships, locations and symbols matter more because the films spend time establishing them before the war reaches its largest scale.
