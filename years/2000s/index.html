How to explore the decade

For fantasy

Start with The Fellowship of the Ring or Spirited Away.

For puzzles

Memento and The Prestige are the clearest mystery-driven options.

For crime

The Dark Knight and No Country for Old Men take very different approaches to threat and morality.

For animation

Spirited Away and WALL·E show two contrasting styles of visual storytelling.
