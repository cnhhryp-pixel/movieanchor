DECADE GUIDE

1970s movies

The 1970s section currently centers on crime, urban drama, early blockbuster suspense and foundational science fiction. It is a smaller catalog than later decades, but several of these films strongly influenced what came after.

How to explore the decade

Crime and power

Start with The Godfather for institutional power or Taxi Driver for a much more subjective urban experience.

Suspense

Jaws is the clearest route into mainstream suspense and creature-driven tension.

Science fiction

Star Wars and Alien show two completely different directions: mythic adventure versus confined horror.

Legacy

Many later MovieAnchor pages connect back to these films through directors, franchises and genre conventions.
