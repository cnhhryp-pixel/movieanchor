How to explore the decade

Crime

Start with Goodfellas, Pulp Fiction or Fargo.

Science fiction

Jurassic Park, Terminator 2 and The Matrix show three very different blockbuster directions.

Drama

The Shawshank Redemption and Saving Private Ryan are the most serious character-driven choices.

Romance and animation

Titanic and Toy Story are two defining mainstream hits from very different genres.
