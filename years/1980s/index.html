How to explore the decade

For action

Start with The Terminator, Aliens or Raiders of the Lost Ark.

For science fiction

Blade Runner, E.T. and Back to the Future show three very different approaches.

For horror

The Shining is the decade’s strongest psychological-horror starting point in the current index.

For animation

My Neighbor Totoro is a calm counterpoint to the decade’s action-heavy blockbusters.
