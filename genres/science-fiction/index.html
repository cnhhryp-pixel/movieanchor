GENRE GUIDE

Science Fiction movies

Science fiction works best when the speculative idea changes the human problem. Use this guide to browse by the kind of question you want the movie to explore.

Choose the kind of science fiction you want

Reality-bending

Start with Inception or The Matrix if understanding reality is part of the conflict.

Space and survival

Try Interstellar, The Martian or Alien depending on whether you want emotion, problem-solving or horror.

First contact

Arrival focuses on language and time, while Contact emphasizes science, evidence and belief.

Artificial life and identity

Blade Runner 2049, Ex Machina and Her each ask different questions about personhood and artificial intelligence.

How to pick your next sci-fi movie

If you want spectacle with clear rules, start with Inception or The Matrix. If you prefer slower, more reflective science fiction, choose Arrival or Blade Runner 2049. For practical survival, The Martian is the most accessible option. For a larger political world, Dune is the strongest starting point.

Science fiction overlaps heavily with thriller, horror, romance and drama. That overlap is useful because the speculative idea often becomes more interesting when it intensifies another familiar kind of story.
