What kind of action do you want?

Contained action

Start with Die Hard for clear geography, improvisation and a single-location conflict.

Science-fiction action

The Matrix, Terminator 2 and Aliens combine technology with pursuit or survival.

Epic action

Gladiator and Dune: Part Two tie action to leadership and political consequence.

Pure momentum

Mad Max: Fury Road is the clearest choice for visual storytelling and sustained movement.
