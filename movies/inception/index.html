MOVIE · 2010

Inception

A skilled extractor who steals information through shared dreams is offered a chance to clear his past by doing the reverse: planting an idea inside a target’s mind.

Why it stands out

Inception combines a heist structure with layered dream logic, making exposition, action and uncertainty part of the same design. Its central questions revolve around memory, grief, perception and whether certainty is possible.
