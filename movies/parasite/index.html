Parasite

Members of a struggling family gradually take jobs in a wealthy household, creating a fragile arrangement built on concealment.

DramaThrillerSpoiler-light guide

What Parasite is about

Parasite begins with a simple opportunity: one member of the Kim family is recommended as a tutor for the wealthy Park household. That opening grows into a coordinated plan as other family members enter the home under separate identities.

The film constantly changes tone without losing control. Comedy, suspense and tragedy all emerge from the same social setup, and physical spaces inside the house become part of the class structure.

Why it works

Few films make architecture carry so much meaning. Stairs, windows, basements and elevation repeatedly show who has privacy, security, visibility and power.

Good fit for: viewers who enjoy dark comedy, social thrillers, escalating tension and films where visual design carries as much meaning as dialogue.

Key themes and ideas

Class and access

Characters are separated not only by money but by space, opportunity and the ability to recover from disaster.

Performance and identity

Nearly everyone performs a role for someone else, whether for employment, status or survival.

Invisible labor

Comfort for one household depends on work and hardship that can remain socially out of sight.

What to watch for

Vertical movement matters. Characters are constantly climbing or descending, mirroring changes in status, security and control.

Parasite FAQ

Is Parasite a horror movie?

Not primarily. It is best described as a drama and thriller with dark comedy and some intense sequences.

Why does the house matter so much in Parasite?

Its layout turns class hierarchy into physical space. Who lives above, below, inside and outside becomes part of the story’s meaning.

What should I watch after Parasite?

Get Out is an excellent next choice for social tension expressed through genre, while Fargo is a good option for crime mixed with dark humor.
